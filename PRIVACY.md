## 隐私政策

知了猴输入法不收集或上传数据。应用不申请网络权限，无法联网。本版本不包含语音输入或录音功能。

- 你输入的内容、Rime 用户数据、剪贴板记录和应用设置都保存在设备本地。
- 应用不读取密码等敏感输入框中的文本。Rime 自身的行为还受你安装的方案和配置影响。
- 只有主动使用“备份全部”时，数据才会写入你选择的位置。备份文件未加密，并包含 Rime 用户数据；请妥善保管。
- 卸载应用会删除其应用目录中的数据。

本政策如有变动，会在此更新。

## Privacy Policy

Youmo IME does not collect or upload data. It requests no network permission and cannot connect to the internet. This build has no speech input or recording feature.

- Text you enter, Rime user data, clipboard history, and app settings stay on the device.
- The app does not read text from password and other sensitive fields. Rime behavior also depends on the schema and configuration you install.
- Data is written to a location you choose only when you use “Back up all”. The archive is unencrypted and includes Rime user data; keep it safe.
- Uninstalling the app deletes data in its app directories.

Changes to this policy will be made here.
